Foundation & Leadership

Jamiyat Welfare Foundation Trust

Managed By Molana Arshad Madni

We JAMIYAT WELFARE FOUNDATION TRUST, (Managed By Molana Arshad Madni) organization working to promote peace, tolerance, interfaith harmony and education, engage with young Muslims for religious moderation, development and empowerment, and provide social welfare and promotion of human rights.

Situated at Dandiyawad, Opp. Peer Khum's Masjid in Dariyapur, Ahmedabad, Suffah English School blends rigorous modern academics with timeless prophetic wisdom so students excel both intellectually and morally.

Core Values

The Distinctive Characteristics

Every classroom activity and community initiative at SES is anchored in these six principles.

01

All-embracing and all-encompassing struggle

Continuous commitment to academic excellence, character refinement, and community upliftment.

02

Sincerity of faith and devotion, and spiritualism

Fostering inner purity, gratitude, and devotion alongside scientific curiosity.

03

Piety

Nurturing truthfulness, self-discipline, and noble conduct in every sphere of life.

04

Changing the society for good

Equipping young minds to contribute constructively to social welfare and civic progress.

05

Universalism

Promoting peace, interfaith harmony, human rights, and mutual respect for all people.

06

Propagation of True Islamic Teachings

Sharing authentic values of compassion, moderation, and lifelong pursuit of knowledge.
